 Carbon-aware charging for an electric bus depot 
 Classical optimizer + QAOA in Qiskit, on real Dutch grid data  ·  Team Bountum 
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ 
 This demo runs five steps (about a minute): 
   1  the grid data for one night 
   2  classical baselines on a 16-qubit scheduling problem 
   3  QAOA in Qiskit on the same problem: circuit, training, sampling 
   4  the 100-bus depot, planned by the classical optimizer 
   5  QAOA as the subsolver inside the depot optimizer
